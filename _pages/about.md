I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UCLA, advised by Professor [Nader Sehatbakhsh](https://ssysarch.ee.ucla.edu/nader/). I earned my bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering from Tsinghua University, where I worked with Professor [Sheng Zhou](http://network.ee.tsinghua.edu.cn/shengzhou/).

My research spans machine learning, embedded systems, and robotics. My recent work studies safe and reliable vision-language-action models and the protection of neural networks on edge devices. I have also worked on distributed coded computation.
