I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UCLA, advised by Professor [Nader Sehatbakhsh](https://ssysarch.ee.ucla.edu/nader/). I earned my bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering from Tsinghua University, where I worked with Professor [Sheng Zhou](http://network.ee.tsinghua.edu.cn/shengzhou/), and my master's degree from Penn State University under Professor Peng Liu.

My research spans secure and private machine learning systems, on-device AI, and distributed coded computation. My recent work studies information leakage from protected neural networks and practical ways to protect models in edge-device deployments.
